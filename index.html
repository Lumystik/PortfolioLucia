I am an interaction designer with an unusual range — three years delivering 150+ ad campaigns at Seedtag, a Master's at Politecnico di Milano, and hands-on UX research at Arduino.

My thesis explores designing for transparency during the age of AI in social media. I write the code behind my designs — HTML, CSS, JavaScript — which changes how I think about interaction.

I work best where craft and speed coexist. I have learned to respect brand systems, move under real constraints, and still make things that feel considered.
